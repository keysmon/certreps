# AIP-C01 Study Resources and Readiness

Resource availability checked 2026-11-05. Everything on this page is optional. You can continue directly to practice without completing a course or checklist.

## Use each resource for a clear purpose

Use the [AWS exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/ai-professional-01/ai-professional-01.html) to define scope and the [official preparation catalog](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certification-prep/) to locate AWS learning activities. The free 20-question set and the subscription Official Practice Exam are distinct resources. The [AIP-C01 Official Practice Exam](https://skillbuilder.aws/learn/HNPB91W7JE/official-practice-exam-aws-certified-generative-ai-developer--professional-aipc01--english/WQAJK2UA19) is a useful unfamiliar benchmark; access may require a subscription. An official practice result is still a preparation signal, not a guarantee of the outcome of another exam form.

Third-party resources can complement this path:

- [Tutorials Dojo sampler](https://portal.tutorialsdojo.com/courses/free-aws-certified-generative-ai-developer-professional-practice-exams-sampler/) offers additional practice with timed and review modes; enrollment may be required.
- [Maarek and Kane's course information](https://courses.datacumulus.com/aws-certified-generative-ai-developer-professional/) describes structured teaching across the GenAI application stack.
- [Whizlabs AIP-C01 resources](https://www.whizlabs.com/aws-certified-generative-ai-developer-professional/) describe practice tests and hands-on labs. Review access, lab scope and costs before choosing them.

These links are resources, not endorsements of every answer or claims that a vendor's difficulty is equivalent to AWS. Their private question banks and labs have not been independently audited by CertReps. No purchase is needed to use CertReps' written exercises.

## Check your reasoning

Before relying on a practice score, ask:

1. Was this an unfamiliar question, or did I remember its answer from an earlier run?
2. Can I explain the requirement that rules out each plausible alternative?
3. Have I covered implementation gaps, not just my strongest domain?
4. Can I read the relevant IAM policy, API request, serving configuration or failure log?
5. Can I explain what proves an asynchronous job completed and how failures recover?
6. Have I checked current AWS documentation for model-, Region- or version-dependent claims?

Use first-answer performance and domain coverage together. A small denominator gives limited evidence; repeated questions can improve recall without demonstrating transfer to a new scenario. Difficulty labels and the study target are local learning aids. A high score, including a perfect score, cannot promise an AWS pass or be converted into its scaled result.
